Hello world. This is the first post on my personal site.

For a long time I kept notes scattered across private files, chats, and half-forgotten drafts. This site is where I want to make that thinking public enough to be useful, but still personal enough to stay honest.

## What this site is for

I do not want to lock the site into one niche. I build software, so there will be engineering notes. I build products, so there will be product decisions and trade-offs. I also watch films, read books, take photos, play games, and get obsessed with random things.

The common thread is simple: write when a thought needs a clearer shape.

## What to expect

- **Tech** — debugging notes, implementation details, tools, and architecture.
- **Product** — decisions, compromises, and lessons from shipping.
- **AI** — practical workflows, experiments, and mistakes.
- **Personal notes** — films, books, games, photos, and anything else that sticks.

No fixed schedule. No content calendar. Just a place to think in public.
