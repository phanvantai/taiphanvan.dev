Examino is a B2B SaaS exam platform for English centers in Vietnam, starting with Cambridge YLE-style placement and mock tests.

## Background

Many centers still prepare tests with Word files, spreadsheets, and email. That workflow is slow, fragile, and gives little structured data for teachers or managers.

Examino aims to make test creation, grading, and reporting easier without forcing centers into a heavyweight LMS.
